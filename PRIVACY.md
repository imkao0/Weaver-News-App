# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-14

Weaver News ("we", "our", or "the app") is an open-source news reader. This
policy explains what data the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you
have.

## Information We Collect

Weaver News is designed to minimize data collection.

### Data stored on your device only
- **Reading preferences** — your selected news edition (language and country),
  category selections, and UI settings. Stored locally using Android DataStore.
- **Bookmarks** — articles you save are stored in a local on-device database
  (Room) and never leave your device.

None of this information is transmitted to us or any third party.

### Data provided by third-party services
- **GNews API** — article headlines, images, and metadata are fetched from the
  [GNews](https://gnews.io) service. When you load the feed or search, your
  device makes a network request to GNews. Please review the
  [GNews privacy policy](https://gnews.io) for how they handle requests.
- **Flags** — country flag images are loaded from flagsapi.com.

## Permissions

- **Internet access** — required to fetch news content and images.
- **Post notifications** *(optional)* — used only to alert you about fresh
  headlines if you enable periodic updates. You can revoke this at any time in
  system settings.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal information. The app contains no
advertising SDKs and no third-party analytics trackers in the base build.

## Data Retention & Deletion

All app data is stored locally on your device. You can delete it at any time by
clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

## Children's Privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in this
file with an updated revision date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on the
project's GitHub repository.
